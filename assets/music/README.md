# Background music

Drop royalty-free music beds here (mp3/wav/m4a) and pass one via
`--music assets/music/your_track.mp3`. Tracks are automatically looped or
trimmed to the final video's total duration, ducked under narration via a
sidechain compressor, and faded out over the last couple of seconds
(`audio.music_fade_out_s` in config.yaml).
